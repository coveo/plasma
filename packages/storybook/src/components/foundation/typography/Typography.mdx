## The family

`canada-type-gibson, sans-serif` — headings and body alike.
There is no second family, and no reason to introduce one.

## What not to do

- Do not set a font size in pixels. Pick the step that is closest; if none fits, the layout is the problem.
- Do not use a heading level for its size. The level is document structure, and screen readers navigate by it. Use `Text` with the size you want.
