## What not to do

- Do not import from `@tabler/icons-react` directly. Everything comes through `@coveord/plasma-react-icons`.
- Do not use an icon alone as the only label for an action. `ActionIcon` needs an `aria-label` matching its tooltip.
- Do not recolour an icon outside the palette; an icon takes the colour of its text.
- Do not reach for a custom icon out of habit. Search Tabler by keyword first — the names are not always the obvious ones.
