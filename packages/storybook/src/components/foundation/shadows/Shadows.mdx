## How they are built

Every step stacks three shadows: a wide soft one, a tighter darker one, and a 1px contact shadow.
Elevation comes from the spread, not from opacity.

```css
--mantine-shadow-md:
    0px 10px 10px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05);
```

<Unstyled>
    <Note>
        All five are a black at low opacity, tuned for the light surfaces Plasma renders on. Separating two
        surfaces that are both dark needs a border, not a shadow.
