## The layers

<Unstyled>
    <Layer term="1 · Ramps">
        Ten steps per hue, <code>--mantine-color-blue-0</code> to <code>-9</code>. These are raw values. Reach for a
        role token instead.
    </Layer>
    <Layer term="2 · Roles">
        What a value is for: <code>--mantine-color-text</code>, <code>--coveo-app-background</code>,{' '}
        <code>--mantine-color-error</code>. Most point at a ramp step, so the step can change without every call site
        changing. A few point at another role instead — <code>--coveo-color-text-primary</code> resolves to{' '}
        <code>var(--mantine-primary-color-filled)</code>. This is the layer to use.
    </Layer>
    <Note>
        <strong>Component stylesheets consume roles directly.</strong> <code>Button.module.css</code> references one
        custom property, <code>--coveo-fw-bold</code>, and nothing component-specific — there is no per-component tier
        between the roles and the CSS.
    </Note>
</Unstyled>

## Motion

`--coveo-transition-duration` is `150ms` and `--coveo-transition-function` is `ease-in-out`.
The theme sets `respectReducedMotion`, so animation is suppressed for anyone who asks the OS for it.

Two tokens and one setting are the whole motion story today.
