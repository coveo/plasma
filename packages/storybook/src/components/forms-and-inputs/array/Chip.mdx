# Chip

Compact pill-shaped control for selecting one or more short options.

## Overview

The `Chip` keeps a small choice set visible while giving each option less visual weight than a standard form field.

Use `Chip.Group` to manage related chips as one single-select or multi-select choice set.

## Guidelines

### When to use

- Users choose from a small set of short options.
- All choices fit comfortably in the available space.
- The selection needs less emphasis than a standard form field.

### When not to use

- For long or searchable lists, use a `Select` or `MultiSelect`.
- For choices that need descriptions or standard form behavior, use a `Checkbox` or `Radio`.
- For non-interactive labels or metadata, use a `Badge`.

### Best practices

- Group related choices with `Chip.Group`.
- Make the single-select or multi-select behavior clear from the surrounding context.
- Avoid chip sets that wrap across many lines or compete with primary actions.

### Content guidelines

- Keep labels short enough to remain on one line.
- Use parallel terms that users can compare quickly.
- Give the group a label that explains what the choices control.
