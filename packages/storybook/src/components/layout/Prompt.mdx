# Prompt

Confirmation dialog with semantic intent and focused footer actions.

## Overview

The `Prompt` asks users to confirm or acknowledge an important decision before continuing. Information, success, warning, and critical variants communicate the consequence or tone of that decision.

## Guidelines

### When to use

- Users must confirm a meaningful or consequential action.
- Semantic tone helps communicate information, success, warning, or critical impact.
- The decision has a clear cancel and confirm path.

### When not to use

- The overlay contains a larger form or custom task. Use `Modal` instead.
- The message can remain visible inline. Use `Alert` instead.
- A routine, low-risk action does not need confirmation.

### Best practices

- Use the warning variant when an action needs caution.
- Reserve the critical variant for destructive or high-risk decisions.
- Keep the prompt focused on one decision and place its actions in `Prompt.Footer`.
- Avoid prompts when users need to compare information on the underlying page.

### Content guidelines

- Name the decision in the title.
- Explain the consequences and whether the action is reversible.
- Use a specific confirm label such as **Delete source**, not **OK**.
- Make the safe alternative clear in the cancel label.
