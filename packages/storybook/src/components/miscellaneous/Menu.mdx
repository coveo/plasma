# Menu

Groups contextual commands in a compact dropdown near their trigger.

`Menu` keeps secondary and overflow actions available without giving each action permanent space in the layout.

## Demo

<Canvas of={MenuStories.Demo} />

## Usage

### When to use

- Actions relate to a nearby trigger, selected item, or group of records.
- Secondary commands would compete with more important visible actions.
- Users need a compact list of related commands.

### When not to use

- The action is primary or used frequently. Keep it visible with a `Button` or `ActionIcon`.
- Users need to compare complex options or enter information.
- The choices navigate between major sections.

### Best practices

- Give the trigger a clear label or accessible name.
- Group related actions with labels and dividers.
- Keep important and frequent actions visible outside the menu.
- Explain a disabled item when its requirement or permission isn't obvious.

## Content and copy

- Start item labels with a specific verb, such as **Archive selected**.
- Keep labels concise and make destructive consequences explicit.
- Write disabled tooltips that explain how to make the action available.
