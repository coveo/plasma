# Collaboration and contact

Plasma evolves through collaboration with the teams that use it.
Many improvements, updates, and clarifications begin with someone identifying a gap, raising a question, or suggesting a change.

You don't need a fully developed proposal or a defined solution to contribute.
Choose the option below that best reflects your needs or the stage of your idea.

## Start with a question

<Unstyled>
    <Button.Primary component="a" href={SLACK_CHANNEL_URL} target="_blank" rel="noopener" mb="sm">
        {`#${SLACK_CHANNEL} Slack channel`}
    </Button.Primary>
</Unstyled>

The <SlackChannel /> Slack channel is the easiest way to connect with the Design System team and get guidance before submitting a formal request.

Use the channel to ask about existing components, clarify usage guidelines, discuss potential gaps, or learn how other teams have approached similar challenges.

Many questions can be resolved directly through discussion, without creating a ticket.
If a formal request is needed, the team will help you identify the next steps and provide the context needed to submit it.

The channel is also used to share Design System updates, release announcements, and changelog entries.

## Open a request

<Unstyled>
    <Button.Primary component="a" href="https://url.coveo.com/plasma-request" mb="sm">
        Request form
    </Button.Primary>
</Unstyled>

When you do need something from the team, the form opens in Jira with the **Design System** project and the right issue type already selected.
You only fill in what the request is.

- **[Request a component, a change or a bug fix](https://coveord.atlassian.net/secure/CreateIssueDetails!init.jspa?pid=17244&issuetype=10105)** — Something is missing, something should work differently, or something is broken in a component. You do not have to decide which — we classify it in triage.
- **[Report a problem in the documentation](https://coveord.atlassian.net/secure/CreateIssueDetails!init.jspa?pid=17244&issuetype=10105&labels=documentation)** — The page is wrong, unclear or out of date, and the component is fine. Same form, arriving tagged `documentation` so it is triaged as a docs fix rather than a code change.
- **Fix the documentation yourself** — A typo, a wrong example, a stale link. The **Edit this page** link in any page footer opens the source on GitHub in your browser and turns your edit into a pull request — no local setup, no ticket.

Found it on a specific page?
The **Report a problem** link in that page's footer opens the second form, already tagged and already carrying the page it came from.

### Before you open it

Thirty seconds that save a round trip.
None of these disqualify a request — they change what we do with it, and most of them are worth a message in the channel first.

### What happens next

Once your request is submitted, the Plasma team will review it and guide you through the next steps.

<Unstyled>
    <Steps>
        <Step>
            <strong>Acknowledgment & Review</strong> — we'll review your request, assess the context, and reach out if
            additional information is needed.
        </Step>
        <Step>
            <strong>Evaluation & Direction</strong> — based on the scope and potential impact, we'll determine the most
            appropriate approach:
            <List mt="xs" spacing="xs" size="sm">
                <List.Item>
                    <strong>Plasma team</strong>: The request aligns with shared Design System needs and may be
                    considered for the roadmap.
                </List.Item>
                <List.Item>
                    <strong>Product team</strong>: The solution is better implemented at the product level, with
                    guidance from the Design System team when needed.
                </List.Item>
                <List.Item>
                    <strong>Outside Plasma's scope</strong>: The request falls outside the Design System's
                    responsibilities.
                </List.Item>
            </List>
        </Step>
        <Step>
            <strong>Decision & Next Steps</strong> — we'll document the outcome and its rationale in the ticket,
            including any recommended next steps.
        </Step>
    </Steps>
</Unstyled>

Our commitment is to provide a transparent process and clear decisions, rather than fixed delivery timelines.
