# About Content

"Content" is the information in the UX working alongside the visual design.
It includes any text, images or videos in the Coveo Administration Console interface that help shape how users navigate the product.

## Who is this for?

This style guide is intended for anyone who contributes to the writing, editing, or reviewing of UX content.

This is often:

- Designers
- Developers
- Technical writers

## The words for the words

Content has a vocabulary of its own, and the distinctions matter when you are deciding what
to write: **UI copy** is the text inside a component, **microcopy** is the wider category that
includes tooltips and error messages, and **short-form** and **long-form copy** are told apart
by length and by where they appear.

All of them, plus the rest of the Plasma vocabulary, are defined in the
[Glossary](./?path=/docs/guidance-glossary--docs).
