Restyle `Menu` to match the new designs

Items and the dropdown use 8px padding with the `md` radius, group labels (`Menu.Label`) are uppercase and use the placeholder color, hover uses the default hover color, and disabled items no longer fade with opacity. `Menu.Search`, `Menu.Sub` and a `ScrollArea.Autosize` around the items follow the same styling.
